Party Games – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Party Games ("the app", formerly Audio Reverser) is made by OddlyYogi. This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Microphone and recordings

The app uses your device's microphone only while you are recording in a game (for example, to play your voice backwards). Recordings are processed on your device. In Party mode, a clip you record for a round is sent directly to the other phones in that party over your local Wi‑Fi or hotspot, and is deleted when the round ends. Recordings are never uploaded to the internet or to any server.

Party mode and your local network

Party mode lets phones on the same Wi‑Fi or hotspot play together. The host's phone runs the game and the other phones connect to it directly over the local network. Only what the games need is exchanged between those phones: the name you type, your game moves, cards and votes, and audio clips in Audio Reverse Challenge. Nothing is sent to the internet, to us, or to any third party, and nothing is kept after the party ends. On iPhone the app asks for "Local Network" permission for this.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a party's QR code to join it. Images are processed on your device to read the code and are not saved or sent anywhere.

Recordings you choose to save are stored in your device's Music › Party Games folder (or shared using your device's share menu). You can delete them at any time with your file manager. Temporary recordings made while playing are removed by the app.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, location or any other personal information. The name you type for Party mode is stored only on your phone and shared only with the phones in your party.

Invites and sharing

If you invite friends with a link, the link (which contains your party's local address and code) is shared using the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or Messages.

If you use the Share option, your device's standard share menu sends the file to the app you pick (for example a messaging app). That transfer is handled by that app and its own privacy policy.

Ratings and links

The app may show the Google Play or App Store rating prompt. That prompt is provided by Google or Apple under their privacy policies. Links to the developer's YouTube or Instagram pages open those services outside the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play or App Store page.
